# Voiceover brief

## Final script

Your tests pass. Your CI is green. You merge.

But services you did not open today can still depend on that API.

Impact Gate is designed to read the contract diff, trace callers in the service graph, and surface evidence for review.

When the evidence is incomplete, it says so. It does not turn uncertainty into a clean result.

A private pilot is being prepared. Join the waitlist to try it on your own services.

## Direction

Calm, precise, slightly wry — a senior engineer explaining something to a peer, not a salesperson. Use one continuous voice; choose a male or female voice neutrally during casting. Deliver at a deliberate pace of roughly one hundred thirty to one hundred forty-five words per minute. Keep sentences short. Add a half-beat pause after “before you merge” if that line is used in the final edit.

## Evidence handling

Do not speak a file path, line citation, finding, service name, or rendered comment unless it comes from a successful fresh capture. The current session produced no such evidence because the required runtime was unavailable.

Keep music out from under the two lines that contain the real product evidence once those lines are restored in the edit. The honesty-layer line should have silence underneath it.
